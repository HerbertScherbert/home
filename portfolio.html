Creative Experience. Learning Impact.

Carmen Herbert · 2026

Drawing on two decades of experience across graphic design, multimedia, and digital learning, I design and develop visually engaging learning solutions that balance creativity with technical innovation. Each project reflects a practical, user-centred approach to creating experiences that drive performance and deliver results.

Why This Matters

As technology, products, and customer expectations continue to evolve, the ability to learn quickly has become a competitive advantage. Effective learning experiences help bridge the gap between information and action, enabling people to adapt, grow, and perform at their best when it matters most.

My Approach

I work closely with subject matter experts and stakeholders to transform complex information into engaging learning experiences. Combining learning design with a strong visual design background, I create cohesive solutions that capture attention, support understanding, and deliver meaningful outcomes.

Project Example

This interactive exercise gives learners the opportunity to practise crafting professional email responses to an unhappy luxury traveller. By focusing on empathy, acknowledgement, and customer-focused language, learners develop the skills needed to de-escalate complaints, rebuild trust, and deliver the high standard of service expected within the luxury travel industry.

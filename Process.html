My Learning Design Process

Every learning solution begins with understanding the problem before designing, developing and measuring success.

01

Discover

Understand the business challenge, learner needs and desired outcomes through stakeholder consultation, analysis and research.

02

Design

Create learning objectives, learner journeys, storyboards, interactions and visual concepts.

03

Develop

Build engaging experiences using Storyline, Rise 360, video, animation and multimedia.

04

Launch

Test thoroughly, ensure accessibility and deploy through the appropriate learning platforms.

05

Measure

Analyse feedback, engagement and performance data to continuously improve learning effectiveness.
